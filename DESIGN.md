# Design System: Pacific Steel 5025 Wiki

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Living Engineering Manual"**

The wiki should feel like a dependable internal knowledge system that happens to belong unmistakably to Pacific Steel 5025. Notion supplies the quiet workspace character; VitePress and Read the Docs supply the familiar documentation structure. Real diagrams, procedures, and technical relationships provide the visual interest.

The interface serves readers in classrooms, workshops, and competition pits. It should settle into the background quickly, remain legible under imperfect conditions, and reveal specialist depth without changing visual language.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Quiet, reading-first hierarchy
- Persistent, predictable navigation
- Restrained team identity
- Modular technical content
- Magnetic motion used as state feedback

**The Durable Knowledge Rule.** Visual structure must reinforce knowledge that survives robot and season changes.

**The Quiet Motion Rule.** Motion may orient, reveal, or confirm; it may never delay reading or become the subject.

## 2. Colors

Use a restrained strategy: neutral reading surfaces with Pacific Steel colors assigned to specific jobs. The exact production palette is resolved from the supplied brand kit during implementation.

**The Earned Color Rule.** Team colors identify navigation, interaction, maintenance, or danger. They are never decorative filler.

## 3. Typography

Use one technical humanist sans family across the interface. Semi-expanded forms distinguish headings while the standard width carries navigation, body text, labels, and tables. The exact supplied family and weights are resolved during implementation.

**The One-Family Rule.** Hierarchy comes from width, weight, size, and spacing—not a collection of unrelated typefaces.

## 4. Elevation

The system is flat by default. Whitespace, tonal separation, and fine rules establish depth; shadows are reserved for temporary overlays such as search.

**The Flat Workspace Rule.** If a permanent surface needs a shadow to feel organized, its hierarchy is not finished.

Component patterns will be extracted after the initial VitePress implementation exists.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use a persistent sidebar, a focused reading column, and an optional page outline.
- **Do** begin system pages in whole-team language and progressively reveal specialist detail.
- **Do** use diagrams and procedures to explain real relationships.
- **Do** pair motion with state changes and support reduced motion.

### Don't:

- **Don't** resemble a SaaS dashboard, marketing landing page, generic AI-generated documentation theme, or season-specific robot-code reference.
- **Don't** use repetitive card grids, oversized hero sections, decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive rounding, vague promotional copy, or gratuitous robotics imagery.
- **Don't** animate every section or hide readable content behind an entrance sequence.
- **Don't** use color alone to communicate status or safety.
