# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The wiki serves the entire Pacific Steel 5025 team: new students, returning members, drivers, pit crew, mechanical and electrical specialists, programmers, mentors, and future team leaders. It must remain understandable during self-guided learning, build meetings, troubleshooting sessions, and time-constrained competition work while preserving enough depth for specialists.

## Product Purpose

The wiki is an evergreen, systems-based robotics knowledge library. It teaches concepts, operating practices, design reasoning, diagnostics, and repeatable workflows that remain useful across competition seasons. It should help any team member understand how a robot system works, use it safely, troubleshoot it methodically, and continue into specialist technical detail without depending on one season's robot code.

Success means knowledge survives team turnover, specialists can share durable expertise, new members can orient themselves without constant supervision, and the team can apply documented principles to new robots each season.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and capable. The experience should feel like a professionally maintained corporate knowledge system with the human clarity of Notion and the dependable information architecture of VitePress and Read the Docs. The voice is direct, practical, and confident without becoming formal for its own sake.

## Anti-references

The wiki must not resemble a SaaS dashboard, marketing landing page, generic AI-generated documentation theme, or a season-specific robot-code reference. Avoid repetitive card grids, oversized hero sections, decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive rounding, vague promotional copy, gratuitous robotics imagery, and animation that competes with reading.

## Design Principles

1. Organize knowledge by durable robot systems and transferable practices.
2. Begin with a whole-team explanation, then reveal specialist depth without splitting the subject into disconnected experiences.
3. Make diagrams, procedures, and real technical relationships carry the visual interest.
4. Keep navigation predictable and pages modular so the library can grow without redesign.
5. Use motion as quiet feedback that improves orientation and understanding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, color-independent status and safety cues, descriptive diagram alternatives, and layouts that work across desktop and mobile. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no information or task completion may depend on animation.
