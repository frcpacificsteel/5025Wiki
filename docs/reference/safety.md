# Safety

Safety depends on the whole robot and the people around it: mechanical design, electrical protection, software behavior, operating procedure, and team communication.

## Core principles

- Know who can enable and who can call a stop.
- Treat stored pneumatic, elastic, gravitational, and rotational energy as active hazards.
- Test new motion at conservative limits and within a controlled area.
- Make faults visible and choose safe behavior when information is missing.
- Never depend on software as the only protection against physical injury.

::: danger Stop when uncertain
If the robot's state, energy, or enable path is unclear, stop and make the system safe before continuing diagnosis.
:::
